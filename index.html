FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential answers on banking, Shariah compliance, and consumer loans in Pakistan.

A full-fledged Islamic bank (such as Meezan Bank, Faysal Bank, BankIslami, and Dubai Islamic Bank) operates 100% of its branches, treasury, and capital under strict Shariah supervision with zero conventional interest-bearing activities. In contrast, an Islamic banking window is a segregated, Shariah-compliant branch network operated by a mainstream conventional commercial bank (such as HBL Islamic or UBL Ameen), where capital and operations for Islamic accounts are strictly ring-fenced.

KIBOR (Karachi Interbank Offered Rate) is the benchmark interest rate at which banks in Pakistan lend unsecured funds to each other. In Pakistan, almost all long-term consumer loans (like auto financing and housing finance) use a floating rate formula such as 1-Year KIBOR + Bank Spread (e.g. 2.50%). When KIBOR changes according to State Bank monetary policy, your monthly installment adjusts at each annual or semi-annual reset date.

In Diminishing Musharakah, the bank and customer enter into a joint equity partnership to purchase the property. The asset is divided into ownership units. As the customer, you pay monthly rent to the bank for utilizing its portion of the home, while simultaneously purchasing the bank's equity units each month. As your ownership percentage grows, your monthly rental drops until you own 100% of the property title.

Raast is the State Bank of Pakistan's instant retail payment system. It connects all banks in Pakistan and enables free, instant funds transfers within seconds. You can link your Pakistani mobile phone number directly to your bank account as your Raast ID through your bank's mobile app, allowing anyone to send money directly to your phone number without typing a long IBAN.

Typically, banks require: 1) Valid CNIC or NICOP copy, 2) Proof of income (last 3-6 months bank statement showing salary or business turnover), 3) Salary certificate / pay slips, 4) Proof of residence (recent paid utility bill), and 5) Clean State Bank e-CIB report verifying no past loan defaults.

Conventional Commercial Banks in Pakistan

Pakistan's flagship commercial banks providing consumer loans, trade finance, corporate treasury, and specialized Islamic window branch operations.

Understanding Islamic Window Operations in Conventional Banks

Under State Bank of Pakistan IBD regulations, conventional commercial banks are permitted to offer Shariah-compliant retail and corporate products via dedicated Islamic Banking Windows (IBWs). These operations maintain completely segregated accounting pools, separate physical branches or counters, and independent Shariah resident board approvals.

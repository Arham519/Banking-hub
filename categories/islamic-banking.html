Shariah Compliant Only

Full-Fledged Islamic Banks in Pakistan

These financial institutions operate 100% of their balance sheet, treasury, and branches strictly on Shariah principles free of Riba (interest), Gharar (speculation), and Maysir (gambling).

Regulatory Clarification: Full Islamic Banks vs. Islamic Windows

In accordance with the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) Islamic Banking Department regulations, a strict distinction is maintained between:

  • Full-Fledged Islamic Banks: Banks like Meezan Bank, Faysal Bank, BankIslami, Dubai Islamic Bank, MCB Islamic, and AlBaraka that have no conventional interest operations whatsoever.
  • Conventional Banks with Islamic Windows: Conventional banks (e.g., HBL, UBL, Bank Alfalah) that operate independent Islamic branches alongside their conventional operations. These windows are listed under their respective banks.
